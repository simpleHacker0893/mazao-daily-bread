Frequently Asked Questions
Is it free? +
Yes, Mazao is completely free to start for individual market traders, mama mbogas, and fundis.
Does it work without internet? +
Yes. Every record saves directly inside your phone's memory. You don't need bundles or Wi-Fi to use it all day.
What if I lose my phone? +
Whenever your phone catches internet, your records back up securely. Sign in on a new phone with your number and PIN to restore everything.
Can I use it for a boda or repair business? +
Yes, Mazao supports service bookings, repair job deposits, fuel expenses, and trip counts just as smoothly as retail produce.
Is it in Kiswahili? +
Titles now, full Kiswahili soon.
